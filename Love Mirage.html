LOVE MIRAGE

Drawing on Indian artisanal heritage and the principle of saṃskāra, the refinement of form through deliberate, skilled gestures, each piece is meticulously constructed around the stone. The result is a singular high-jewelry creation, designed to preserve the stone’s identity while expressing the highest standards of contemporary craft. Within the Love Mirage journey, every creation begins with a personally selected stone, whose qualities determine the architecture of the final design. Guided through a bespoke one-to-one process, clients follow each stage of craftsmanship: from the initial assessment of the gem to the patient shaping, setting, and engineered faceting that define its character.

HARSSH DOSHI is a 2025 - born Indian luxury jewellery house positioned at the intersection of deep Indian heritage and global luxury language. The brand is not a reiteration of traditional Maharashtrian jewellery; Instead, it represents a refined and modern Indian identity akin to what Nirav Modi accomplished. The goal is to speak to contemporary Indians across the globe, especially the diaspora, who seek cultural proximity without compromising on modern elegance.
