WORLD OF HARSSH DOSHI

HARSSH DOSHI is a contemporary Indian fine and high jewelry house shaped by a vision of modern Indian elegance, expressed through craftsmanship, heritage, and refined design. Rooted in India’s artisanal legacy, particularly that of the Gujarat region, the House approaches tradition as a living foundation rather than a fixed reference. Cultural symbolism, proportion, and material intelligence are translated into precise, contemporary forms through a disciplined and measured process. Each creation is crafted with respect for time, mastery, and the human hand, treating gold and precious stones as enduring elements shaped to gather meaning over years of wear. Conceived as a House built on continuity and restraint, HARSSH DOSHI creates jewelry designed to accompany a life and mature with it.

LOVE MIRAGE

Follow each stage of craftsmanship behind your Love Mirage creation, from the initial assessment of a stone selected for its character to the patient shaping, setting, and engraving that define its form. Guided through a bespoke one-to-one process, the stone becomes the centre of the design, refined through disciplined techniques and careful handwork. Each detail is developed in dialogue with the client, resulting in a singular high-jewelry creation shaped to preserve the stone’s identity and endure over time.
