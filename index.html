WORLD OF HARSSH DOSHI

HARSSH DOSHI is a contemporary Indian fine and high jewelry house, born from a vision to express a modern Indian identity through craftsmanship, heritage, and refined design. Rooted in India’s artisanal legacy, particularly that of the Gujarat region, the House approaches tradition as a living foundation, translating cultural symbolism and material intelligence into precise, contemporary forms. Each creation is shaped through a meticulous process that honours time, mastery, and the human hand, treating gold and precious stones as carriers of meaning.
