BANDHARAN

Rooted in the Gujarati word बंधारण, Bandharan draws inspiration from India’s timeless symbolism of unity and interconnectedness. Each creation is meticulously crafted, where interlaced gold forms and luminous diamonds are patiently refined to echo this heritage. Through every step, the links gain brilliance and harmony, revealing a sculptural elegance that honours tradition with contemporary craftsmanship.
