Our Legacy

For over three decades, Harshh Doshi has been crafting extraordinary pieces that tell stories of love, tradition, and timeless beauty. Each creation is a testament to our commitment to excellence and artisanal mastery.

Craftsmanship

Our skilled artisans combine traditional techniques with contemporary design, creating jewellery that transcends time. From delicate filigree work to bold statement pieces, every detail is meticulously crafted.

Premium Materials

We source only the finest gold, precious stones, and diamonds, ensuring that each piece not only captivates with its beauty but also endures for generations to come.

Bespoke Experience

Our personalized approach ensures that every piece is as unique as the person who wears it. From initial consultation to final creation, we work closely with you to bring your vision to life.

WORLD OF HARSSH DOSHI

HARSSH DOSHI is a 2025-born Indian luxury jewellery house positioned at the intersection of deep Indian heritage and global luxury language. The brand is not a reiteration of traditional Maharashtrian jewellery; instead, it represents a refined and modern Indian identity akin to what Nirav Modi accomplished. The goal is to speak to contemporary Indians across the globe, especially the diaspora, who seek cultural proximity without compromising on modern elegance.
